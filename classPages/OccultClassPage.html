The Occult Classes


Base Classes
The Occult classes represent those who engage with fringe magic, interacting with the aether in ways distinct from the traditional methods. For players, be warned that these classes tend to be significantly more complex than most other classes. In addition, the power levels of these classes vary wildly, and are not well balanced.

These classes are suggested for players who are willing to put significant homework and reading into understanding a strange and not-necessarily strong class in order to engage with the world in unique ways.

Occultist
The poster child of Occult Classes, the occultist reads the history of objects and can pull power from special artifacts known as Implements. In TWE, Implements can appear in all sorts of places for all sorts of reasons, often harboring hidden power only accessible to Occultists. This class is not very strong.

Kineticist
The kineticist channels the power of the elements to unleash consistent, incredibly powerful blasts at their foes. They use their constitution as a power source, making them tough and deadly. They do not cast spells, but they are terrifyingly strong in combat.

Medium
Mediums are versatile spirit channelers, who can channel different spirits each day to fill virtually any party role.

Mesmerist
Mesmerists use their psychic power to excel in enchantment and illusion, tricking and manipulating the minds of their foes toward their own ends.

Psychic
Psychics are full casters, like wizards or sorcerers, but they use their minds to cast spells. They replace Verbal, Somatic, and Material components with entirely nontangible emotion-based components, and can choose a discipline to channel their phrenic powers. A powerful mentalist wielding Intelligence alongside either Wisdom or Charisma.

Spiritualist
Highly spiritual beings with the ability to interact with spirits of the dead and ethereal. They have a powerful Phantom they can conjure at any time.
