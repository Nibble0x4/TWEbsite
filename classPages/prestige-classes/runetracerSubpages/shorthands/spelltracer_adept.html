Description
You have studied spellcasting intently, advancing your understanding of how to capture arcane spells in the form of runes.

Prerequisites:
Spelltracing.

Benefit:
Gain an additional +4 bonus on spellcrack checks made to spelltrace. Tracing a spell takes 2 actions instead of the usual 3.
