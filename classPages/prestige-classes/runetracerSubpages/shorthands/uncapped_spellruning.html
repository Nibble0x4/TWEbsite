Description
You have learned how to use runes to break the limits on weaker spells.

Prerequisites:
Runetracer 3.

Benefit:
When you use Spellruning to increase the Caster Level of the spell, if there is a maximum on how high the caster level can boost the spell, increase that limit by your Runetracer Level + 1.
