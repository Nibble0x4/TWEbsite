Description
Your mastery of Adjectives allows you to refine your runes even further.

Prerequisites:
Descriptive Writer.

Benefit:
You may use a Rune as an Adjective to another rune as a swift action whenever you trace.
If you spend 1 extra action while tracing, you may instead apply 2 Adjectives.
