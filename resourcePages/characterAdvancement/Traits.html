Traits!


Traits are an optional system that you may engage with in order to put a little bit more spice or personality into your character.

Traits offer small bonuses, little +1s and +2s and -1s that can be annoying to track and offer little in terms of power. However, if you are trying to maximize power, then this isn't really a resource intended for you.

The primary purpose of Traits is to provide players a way to manifest a character aspect or personality quirk as a numerical bonus or effect in-game. This is intended to help the player cauterize the personality of their character in a visual way.

Unless otherwise told by the DM, a new character is free to select up to 2 Traits total, as long as they meet the requirements.

The Flow


Personality Traits


These are the basic kinds of traits, reflective of what you are actually like! They add small bonuses to help give weight to your personality and assist with roleplay.



Racial Traits


Some races have traits specific to them, some characteristics that tend to appear in some of their number without being true for all of that race. (This list is not comprehensive at the moment.)



Faith Traits


These are traits you can select if you revere a specific deity! They reflect a deep, personal, intrinsic reverence for a deity based on who they are and what they do, not just what they can do for you.



Combat Traits


These traits reflect what kind of person you are in combat, or at least how you handle confrontation. While they give benefits in battle, try to remember that traits you pick here should also be reflected in your combat decisions.
