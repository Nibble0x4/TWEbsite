Bazaar


Bazaars are exciting places full of the strange and mystical. Most normal people love the break from mundanity when they visit these places, but they are not always available! If you see one outside of a city, you may wish to take advantage!

Bazaars are full of a seemingly random variety of objects, making standardized shopping difficult, but there are categories that items tend to fall under. Use this page to investigate ahead of your next Bazaar visit!

Don't forget to select your settlement to get adjusted prices and proper currencies!
