Boutique


Boutiques are stylish locales where adventurers are often outnumbered by normal citizens! Boutiques exist in some shape or form in any settlement, though the form they take can range from a lone needlewoman to a full-fledged fashion-forward establishment!

Boutiques sell clothes. These clothes usually do nothing to protect you in combat, but they are comfortable and stylish and can provide social benefits in some situations! Use this page to see how to fill your wardrobe so you are ready with a matching set for any occasion!

Don't forget to select your settlement to get adjusted prices and proper currencies!
