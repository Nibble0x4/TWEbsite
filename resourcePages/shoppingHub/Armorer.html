Armorer


Armorers are essential stops for any adventuring crew. In a world as dangerous as TWE, it is important for most people to have some sort of weapon at their side. Blacksmiths forge standard equipment and custom equipment alike, if you have the coin! The key is to find a skilled enough smith for the item you desire.

Picking the right equipment can mean the difference between death and survival, so be sure to choose carefully. Use this page to see what exactly you can afford!

Don't forget to select your settlement to get adjusted prices and proper currencies!
