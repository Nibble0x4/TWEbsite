Docks


Docks exist in all ports of TWE, and most settlements have a marketplace equivalent. Either way, this is the place where most merchants make their money!

Most merchants make money through the buying and selling of general goods. Materials that can be acquired cheaply in one place and sold for a profit elsewhere. Use this page to explore costs surrounding different goods throughout TWE!

Don't forget to select your settlement to get adjusted prices and proper currencies!
