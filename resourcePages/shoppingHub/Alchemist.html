Alchemist


Alchemists are specialized masters of brewing, spending their days gathering, tending, mixing, grinding, and distilling herbs to bring you incredible concoctions! They can be found in cities for the most part, but have been seen in smaller settlements as well.

Use this page to see how much you can expect to pay for the most standard potions! Some alchemists can imitate spells with their potions, so you can also see how much that might cost you!
