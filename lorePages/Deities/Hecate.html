Hecate, Mistress of Magic


Hecate is the goddess of magic, mistress of the arcane, and governess of aether. She is the weaver of spells, the keeper of all the magic known to the residents of TWE. She is a powewrful and zealous defender of Taria, raising magical militia to protect their homeland.
Under her authority, magic is restrained or empowered. Some magics have, at her will, been determined to be too dangerous or too twisted to be allowed to run rampant in TWE. However, in her new state, arcana has entered a more flexible, mixable state.



Status

Hecate is enshrined as the Secondary Goddess of Taria.

Personality
Hecate is a goddess of power. She holds the secrets of the arcane, to be wielded for her own purposes. She is a powerful patron goddess of Taria, the one who empowers the weak of body and the downtrodden.
She was once cold and distant, hording her mysteries and looking down on the mortals that filled her world, but since returning she has changed. Now she sees the small, the overlooked and those with no other hope.
She is regal in presence, but has a warm side that she only shows to the weak. Her power is vast and her gaze cold to the outsider, driven by her fierce protective love for her people.

Creed
To ever grow in power and understanding of the Aether. To seek out its secrets and consider its ramifications. To see magic clearly, with eyes untinged by the potential and focused on the tangible. To recognize the existence of evil both in people and the magic they weave from the aether. To cast aside the use of magic for cruelty or domination, and to embrace its power as both weapon and barrier. To grow others along with yourself, understanding the increasing responsibility that comes to those with arcane power and to rise to meet those responsibilities. To upkeep knowledge, safeguard history, and invest in the future of the Tarian people. To see magic as more than just power or a tool, but to appreciate the beauty, hope, and possibilities it brings. All the while, remember that the Aether is sacred. Its power is real and wild, destructive if wielded flippantly. To be humble in your wielding of it, careful and aware of the magnitude of what you do. As you do so, know that your goddess holds your power in her hand and, if you should fail, will tear it from you and cast you aside.

Becoming a Follower
The path to becoming a true follower of a deity is a journey in and of itself. Doing so requires journeying to the deity's shrine and performing acts of devotion that endear you to them.

When you have proven yourself and gained their favor, you may choose to subject yourself to their Creed and become their follower. Doing so grants you Divine Blessings and a Divine Power. As you continue to follow them faithfully, always living by the Creed, your deity level increases (this level is separate from your character and class levels) and new Divine Powers are attained.

Since Hecate has been enshrined again, properly becoming her follower no longer requires being a cleric.

However, if you break the Creed, you will suffer Divine Judgment from your deity until you atone. Be very careful.

Shrine Location

Hecate's shrine is somewhere in the Uncrossable, in Taria.

Acts of Devotion

Hecate desires her followers to grow ever stronger in the understanding and wielding of powerful magic, using it to protect her people.
To catch her attention, grow in arcane prowess and wield it for the betterment of the weak.
To find others who are abusing magic and stop them by being better.
Finally, to wield your magic unyieldingly against those who must be stopped.

Divine Blessings

These blessings are gained by every new official follower upon their acceptance by the goddess and their sworn adherence to the Creed.

  1. Gain a +1 Divine Bonus to Intelligence or Charisma, your choice.
  2. Gain a +1 Divine Bonus to any ability score.
  3. Gain a +2 Divine Bonus on Knowledge(Arcana) checks.
  4. Gain a +1 bonus to all DCs for spells you cast.
  5. Gain access to the Detect Magic cantrip as a spell-like ability.
  6. Gain a Spellcraft as a class skill. If it already was, then gain a +3 divine bonus to it.

Clerics of Hecate
It is not necessary to be a Cleric to follow Hecate. However, if you wish to begin play as a Cleric who worships the goddess of wisdom, then you may choose to live by her creed and begin as a follower of Hecate. Doing so will grant you all the benefits of Hecate Level 1. Here is some information specific for clerics of Hecate.

Available Domains
Any cleric of Hecate must have at least one of these domains. Any domain spells or powers from these domains used by the cleric gains +1 Caster Level.

Air-Lightning, Artifice-Alchemy, Artifice-Construct, Artifice-Toil, Chaos-Entropy, Charm-Captivation, Darkness-Night, Destruction-Hatred, Fire, Glory-Legend, Good-Azata, Good-Redemption, Healing-Restoration, Knowledge, Law, Liberation, Luck-Curse, Luck-Fate, Madness-Insanity, Magic, Protection-Defense, Ruins, Rune-Legislation, Rune-Wards, Trickery-Greed, Water-Ice

Favored Weapon

Hecate favors the Ritual Knife, a weapon that was made to serve magical ends.

Channeling Effect

Whenever a cleric (or paladin) channels the power of Hecate, it comes with an additional effect depending on whether the channel is being used to heal or to harm. These effects often incorporate X, where X is the cleric's Hecate level:
Healing - All affected creatures are granted a +X bonus to their will saves against spells. If they are casters, they can choose to consume this bonus upon casting a spell to increase their effective caster level by 1/2X.
Harming - Any creature that has at least 1 arcane spell prepared can also be harmed by this channel. If they would already be harmed by this channel, they take an additional X force damage.
