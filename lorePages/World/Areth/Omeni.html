The City of Omeni




Size and Population


Class 2 - Large City
Omeni is a sprawling city with a tight, efficiently laid-out downtown core with vertical construction. Outside of this inner core, the building sizes slope downward as efficient city planning turns into more varied and relaxed construction that emphasizes beauty over practicality.

Many kinds of people live in Omeni, making it perhaps the most diverse city in Areth. Military operatives live alongside workers, merchants, nobles, commoners, and tourists alike.
Most common races:
Human(Arethian), Gnome, Elf, Halfling, Kitsune, Hamo-Buru
Races more common than normal:
Changeling, Pixie, Sprite
Races less common than normal:
Dwarf, Ifrit, Undine

Placement and Area
Omeni is built near the edge of the Fae Woods, northwest of Lake Divinity. The town center is very close to the forest, but most expansion has pushed along and away from the Fae Woods for the safety of the citizens.

Omeni does not dedicate much land to agriculture, but some food production does exist in the southern reaches of the city.

Omeni is positioned near large notable landmarks, the Fae Woods to their northwestern border and the Blight a few days' travel to the southeast.

History
Omeni was originally founded as a military encampment under the Ichiheibai dynasty to help Kyomana hold the northern border in Imperial Year 241. The Fae Woods act as a natural barrier, but in the early days of the empire extra assistance was needed in keeping the Grussian barbarians out.

Over time, as Kyomana and the northern front strengthened, Omeni began to change. The shrine of Fu-Xing was constructed in Imperial Year 413, early in the Fuheibai dynasty. This marked a notable shift, as Omeni became less militaristic under her guidance. While it still did its duty, the city which had gone generations without being attacked opened itself up to different kinds of people. Commoners came and brought with them art, music, and a diverse set of backgrounds. All who came were welcomed under Fu-Xing's teachings, leading to an unprecedented explosion of growth in population and culture.

Today, Omeni is the second largest and single most diverse city in Areth, boasting thousands of years of culture and a storied history. Many of the old military districts have been retired and converted into museums of the city's storied history.

Some Factions
Followers of Fu
- Devotees of Fu, the goddess of love and beauty.
This faction is large and pervasive. They are based in the shrine of Fu-Xing, but they spread all throughout Omeni. Single and bands of these people can be seen roving around in search of matches to be made. Lonely or passionate individuals are their favorite targets.
This faction does not control territory beyond their base, but they are also not restricted to territory. They are considered harmless and attacking them is unwise, as other factions and common people hold them in high esteem.

Followers of Xing
- Devotees of Xing, the god of familial bonds and companionship.
This faction is large and pervasive. They are based in the shrine of Fu-Xing, but they spread all throughout Omeni. Single and bands of these people can be seen roving around in search of the lonely and jaded.
This faction does not control territory beyond their base, but they are also not restricted to territory. They are considered harmless and attacking them is unwise, as other factions and common people hold them in high esteem.

Kuheibai
- Extended members and supporters of the Kuheibai, the current ruling dynasty.
This faction has great power in town through their connections. They are treated almost like royalty and enjoy a degree of legal immunity.

The Faewing Guild
- An organized group of skilled explorers, warriors, mages, and merchants specializing in the Fae Woods.
This group has existed for several generations now, fostering respect, understanding, and healthy fear of the wild Fae Woods north of the city.
They do not control territory, but they have a headquarters and several outposts throughout town for use in their work.
They are a respected cultural group.

Jonheibai
- A very young and new branch of the Heibai family slowly growing in influence in the city.
This branch is matriarchal, claiming Fu as a patron deity, and they have gained some power by aligning themselves with Empress Dowager Ue to promote female leadership.
This group has been controversial, playing a delicate dance of not stepping on the toes of Kuheibai and Aneome.

Omeni Military
- A branch of the Arethian military.
This faction has marked off several sectors of Omeni for official military purposes. Those sectors are highly classified and are actively patrolled.
This faction has converted a sector into a historic sector dedicated to education and military history.
The Omeni Military answers to the governing family of Omeni, Aneome, and the Kuheibai.

Aneome Family
- The historic ruling family in charge of managing Omeni in service of the Imperial Family.
This faction has ultimate authority in Omeni, but is known for being lenient and benevolent. They are well-loved and respected.
The family sponsors the arts and the cultural advancements of their city, believing such things to be the signs of a truly successful city.

Some Points of Interest

Shrine of Fu-Xing
The seat of power for the goddess of love.
A shining white temple decorated with flowering gardens. All are welcome to stroll the area and listen to sermons. Public Hot Spring baths and temporary lodgings are available here free of charge for the needy.
Followers of Fu and Xing mingle freely here with little distinction, tending to and tempting those who enter the grounds.

Omeni Military History Museum
A museum showcasing artifacts, weapons, and stories from Omeni's storied military past.
Entrance is free for locals. A common gathering place for followers of Xing.
It is a common place for social activism

Yasuru tavern
A standardized tavern and inn, run by the highly successful Nerusumu family.
This is a well-respected tavern popular with travelers. Yasuru taverns can be found all around Areth with standardized fare, rates, and quality levels.
Travelers recognize the name and know that it means quality. The tavern has strong anti-violence policies that keep shadier figures away.
A good place to find tasks for traveling adventurers.

Aineru tavern
A popular tavern for locals, not often spoken of but well known.
This tavern is beloved by the locals. It is cheaper and of good quality, but has no restrictions on patronage.
Acts done to damage this tavern will be noted by the locals, as the Meromo family who runs it is well loved.
A good place to find rumors.

Utsukubasho
The biggest market in the city.
This is the primary shopping place for fine and artisanal goods. Prices are high, but most people there are ready and willing to pay for something that stands out.
There are several stages in the market, available for those who coordinate to perform on them.

Shopping

Eager shoppers will find pretty much anything they need in Omeni, if they are willing to search for it.
Magic is strong in the city, with a particularly powerful fae influence. Magical items of many kinds are available, both standard and niche.
Quality craftsmanship is prized in Omeni, with exceptional emphasis on Arethian weapons and many Exotic weapon types.
Due to the strong emphasis on culture and craftsmanship, many items are slightly more expensive here than they would be in other settlements.

For those looking to sell their items, Omeni will offer fair rates. A savvy merchant will learn the history of the items in their stock and find that that will sell faster than quality.

Exports

Omeni's primary exports are artistic pieces, masterwork Arethian military-grade equipment, and fae-touched magical items.
Most of Omeni's bulk exports travel to Omeni-Ka for trade or transport to Yanor.

-Ka

Omeni has a Ka settlement south of it, on the border of Lake Divinity.

Omeni-Ka is a small trading port that allows the city to more easily move goods between settlements, especially to Yanor or far away cities like Arkei or Ekesh.
Omeni-Ka is the quickest-growing Ka in Areth, serving both Omeni and Plota. This also makes Omeni-Ka the best suited to hosting travelers.

Omeni-Ka's selection will be more limited than Omeni's, but the prices there will be significantly better.
