The Arethian Empire


Areth is the Eastern Province, inspired by Japan and ancient Chinese influences. It is a vibrant and beautiful place, blessed with abundance and ruled by imperial dynasties with strong respect for tradition and culture.

This page gives a basic overview of the Arethian Province, including its history, structure, priorities, prime exports, religions, geography, cities, culture, traditions, and political relations.

History


Founding
From time immemorial, Areth has been a land set apart, a world nestled between the deadly extremes of darkness and light, bursting with life and promise. However, it wasn't until Heibai Arrein, the legendary warrior patriarch of the Heibai family, that the land was truly united as one under the auspices of the sun god Xihe.

Songs and legends aplenty describe the battles that Arrein and his family fought, the war they waged against the darkness and ignorance of barbarians that sought to claim the land for their own. However, with the power of Xihe and the other gods behind them, civilization prevailed. The people rallied beneath the Heibai, cheering as they took their heavens-ordained place as the imperial family and generously spread their light to every corner of the blessed lands.

The land set apart, already teeming with life, reacted to the divine decree of the gods and rewarded the good people of Areth with Flux, a vibrant and potent magical power that flowed only through their soil, empowering their people and serving as a proof of the divine authority given to the imperial family.

Unlike the greedy kings of the west, the Arethian empire was named not after its humble founders, but for the dark world it overcame in its founding. In the east, the term 'aretsu' is used to refer to difficult times, reminding its citizens of the hard, dark times that came before the light of the sun arrived.

With very little interruption, the Heibai family has continued to serve in their divine task, guiding and protecting the Arethian people.

Over the Years
Areth has flourished under the Heibai family's rule. Different dynasties have risen and fallen within the family, marking periods where the imperial line put more emphasis on different aspects of Arethian life and development, such as the Ichiheibai dynasty's focus on the esablishment of Arethian borders and the province's new glorious capitol city.

The Fuheibai dynasty saw the construction of new shrines for her deities, couching them in new settlements worthy of their divinity.

The Miheibai dynasty famously turned their focus outward from the majestic city of Yanor, growing and cultivating the unique settlements and subcultures of Arkei, Omeni, and Plota.

Under the Yotsuheibai dynasty, the borders were more greatly contested. The imperial family rallied its people, devising great advancements in military and establishing two powerful military bases to the north and south, Kyomana to push back the mighty northern barbarians and Ekesh to protect the beaches from barbarian sailing vessels.

The Goheibai dynasty was beloved for their participation in and support of cultural and artistic endeavors. The Great Library is established under their rule and historic texts gathered there.

The Rokuheibai dynasty strengthened the borders greatly, amassing the strength necessary to form the sea blockade and doubling efforts to push the barbarians of the north back into Gruss. They also repelled an attack by the new Enthan Empire, declaring them greedy westerners deemed unfit by the gods.

The Nanaheibai dynasty was characterized by a spirit of adventure, advising their citizens to learn magic, train with the blade, and explore their beautiful lands for themselves. This caused some dissent, but was a period of a great broadening of the minds of the people as they experienced new places and got to know their fellow Arethians better.

The Botatsu dynasty was a dark time. The Heibai family was tricked, overthrown, and banished from Yanor by a greedy family who used the imperial throne to bask in opulence and lavish living. This period was frought with hunger and disease among the poor masses as wealth was funneled to the top. The Blood Goddess appeared during this time and aided Heibai Kudon in his dramatic and heroic rebellion to retake the throne. During the rebellion, the Great Library was burned and untold knowledge lost.

The Kuheibai dynasty has been marked by economic revolution following the bloody retaking of the throne from the Botatsu. Huge shifts in wealth and the construction of new -Ka settlements along with reliable roads and trade routes has given skilled tradesmen and farmers unprecedented success and value. However, imperial police have spread through the land to set about correcting the lies spread by the Botatsu, finding and destroying false texts and those who spread lies that poison the hearts of the commonfolk.

Provincial Relationships
Historically, Areth has a rocky relationship with every other province and culture in That Which Exists. They have always seen themselves as the blessed land and, by contrast the other provinces as barbarians.

Areth has a fiercely hostile relationship with Gruss, having waged war of varying intensity on their northern border for as long as the empire has existed.

Enthan is considered to be the only other civilization in That Which Exists that rivals Areth in power. Areth sees Enthan as their equal and opposite, an immoral and debased earthly empire to contrast their own divine home.

Taria has been historically considered to be barbaric to Areth, though their advancements in magic and technology has led to them seeming more like a prize to claim than a foe to defeat.

Chet'Tun is a small island, not often considered by the larger Arethian Empire.

Areth Today
Areth is shining as brightly as ever, her economy is thriving, her workmen are skilled and appreciated, and her people are well-fed. In fact, her prosperity is so great that the Empress Dowager has revealed her late husband's dying wish: To spread Aeth's divine blessings to the cursed lands of the South.

Emperor Heiwa Heibai was well-known for his internal focus, turning his military attention inward to provide security for trade and citizens, allowing for an unprecedented economic growth spurt that saw the standard of living rise and investments flourish. He was very popular with the people of the capitol and the cities he routinely toured, providing financial assistance to promising mercantile ventures and speaking tirelessly of the golden future he was shown by Xihe.
His passing was a deep blow for the common people of Areth. It was sudden, the result of an illness he had hidden masterfully in order to maintain the image of a glorious leader for the sake of his people. It was with a heavy heart that his wife, Ue, placed his 2-year old son Ixi upon the throne in Imperial Year 3015. While he grows, Ue has accepted the mantel of Empress Dowager as she works to prepare Areth for the crowning of their new Emperor.

In her zeal to see her beloved husband's dream come to fruition, Empress Dowager Ue has turned the military outward, pushing an invasion force southward into Taria. The barbarians there have refused the offer of divinity by repelling the invasion force, disappointing the Empress Dowager, but she has declared that this is to be expected. The ignorant Tarians do not understand what they have denied and will one day be blessed with Aethian guidance.

Religion
In Areth, the deities are believed to be more than singular. Each deity, as perfected beings, possess both masculinity and femininity. Each deity has two faces, two aspects.
Their Primary aspect is either masculine or feminine and represents their most clearly portrayed traits. Most followers of a deity follow the Primary aspect.
Their Secondary aspect is the complement to the Primary and represents the oft-overlooked nuances of the deity. This aspect supports and completes the Primary while providing followers a different angle from which to view the deity.
Followers of a deity will normally revere one aspect over the other, living out their worship in sometimes radically different ways.

Pantheon
The Eastern Empire is a highly religious entity, serving 4 gods who have blessed the empire with the divine right to rule.

The primary deity of Areth is Xihe, the sun god. His shrine is located in Yanor in the Lake.
Those who worship Xihe's masculine Primary aspect often do so out of respect for his divine authority and his fierce spirit.
Those who instead revere Xihe's feminine Secondary aspect tend to do so out of gratefulness for her warm light and protection.

The secondary deity of Areth is Tu-Shen, the goddess of the earth. Her shrine is located in Plota.
Followers of Tu, the feminine Primary aspect, are hard workers who care for honesty and fair rewards.
Followers of Shen, the masculine Secondary aspect, are rare. They are self-motivated and often reclusive or individualistic.

The people's deity of Areth is Fu-Xing, the goddess of love. Her shrine is located in Omeni.
Those who revere Fu, the feminine Primary aspect, usually do so enraptured by the fierce passion of romantic love and intimate attraction.
Those who revere Xing, the masculine Secondary aspect, are oft inspired by the warm, strong bonds of family and friends.

The fringe deity of Areth is Yin-Yang, the twin-deity of balance. Their shrines are located in Arkei.
Followers of Yin, the Feminine aspect of the twin-deity, tend to be orderly people who care about structure, law, and predictability.
Followers of Yang, the Masculine aspect of the twin-deity, tend to be chatoic, unstructured people who act spontaneously and enjoy change.

Cities


Yanor in the Lake
The historic capitol and glorious seat of power for the Imperial family. Home to the shrine of Xihe.

Kyomana
The northwestern city, a highly militaristic settlement dedicated to continuing the border war with Gruss.

Omeni
The northeastern settlement. A sprawling city bordering the Fae Woods and featuring a diverse population. Home to the shrine of Fu-Xing.

Ekesh
The southeastern settlement and military port. A dim and serious locale where only the most committed make their homes.

Arkei
The southeastern city, an enigmatic and occasionally unpredictable place where the population itself seems to be divided on itself. Home to the shrine of Yin-Yang.

Plota
A simple, expansive farming community that serves as a retirement town. Home to the shrine of Tu-Shen.
